# Campus Customs storefront design

- **Collegiate palette:** Deep navy anchors the header, hero, buttons, and chat; warm cream keeps large areas calm; a muted gold accent points to key actions. The contrast makes prices, navigation, and calls to action easy to scan.
- **Editorial hierarchy:** Large serif headlines give the shop a campus publication feel, while compact sans-serif labels identify product types and controls. A two-column hero and generous spacing make the collection feel curated rather than like a database dump.
- **Product-first presentation:** Real catalogue images sit in consistent frames. Cards reveal a detail affordance on hover, and detail pages place a large image beside price, description, and size quantities so shoppers can judge an item quickly.
- **Helpful chat:** The navy and gold floating control stays available across pages. Assistant replies are readable, and matched products appear as compact image cards that open the full item page.
- **Restrained motion:** The hero enters gently, its featured product floats slightly, and the chat panel opens with a short transition. Visible keyboard focus and a reduced-motion rule keep the effects from obstructing navigation.
